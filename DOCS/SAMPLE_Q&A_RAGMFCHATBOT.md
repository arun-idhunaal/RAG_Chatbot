## 2. Non-Scheme Factual Queries

### Q6
**Q:** What is an expense ratio in a mutual fund?

**A:** The expense ratio is the annual operating expense charged by a mutual fund scheme, expressed as a percentage of assets, subject to the applicable rules and disclosures. [Approved non-scheme source citation]

### Q7
**Q:** What is an exit load?

**A:** Exit load is a charge that may apply when units are redeemed within the period and under the conditions specified by a scheme. The exact charge is scheme-specific. [Approved non-scheme source citation]

### Q8
**Q:** What is a mutual fund riskometer?

**A:** A riskometer is a standardized indicator used to communicate the level of risk associated with a mutual fund scheme. [Approved non-scheme source citation]

### Q9
**Q:** How can I download my mutual fund statement?

**A:** Use the applicable statement/download process described in the approved source. The chatbot should provide the supported steps and link to the relevant official page. [Approved non-scheme source citation]

### Q10
**Q:** What is the difference between SIP and lump-sum investment?

**A:** A SIP generally refers to investing a specified amount periodically, while a lump-sum investment refers to investing an amount in a single transaction. [Approved non-scheme source citation]
